System Improvement Report – Week 6 Iteration

Based on system testing and user feedback, several usability and clarity improvements were identified and addressed.

Issues Identified:

* Summaries were occasionally longer than necessary
* Some extracted keywords were non-informative or repetitive
* Segment labels were not always descriptive
* Minor readability inconsistencies in output formatting
* Fast speech caused slightly unclear segments

Improvements Implemented:

* Adjusted summary generation to produce more concise text
* Filtered filler words and non-meaningful keywords
* Improved segment label clarity for better understanding
* Standardized formatting and spacing of outputs
* Enhanced readability of transcript structure

Result After Iteration:
The output became easier to read and quicker to understand.
Users were able to grasp the conversation flow faster and reported improved clarity in summaries and keywords.

Note:
No machine learning models were retrained.
Improvements focused only on presentation, clarity, and usability as per Week-6 requirements.
